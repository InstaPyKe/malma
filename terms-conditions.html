Terms of Service & Engagement

Effective Date: September 2026 • Governing Full-Stack Web Development, Android App Engineering, and 1-on-1 Developer Tutoring.

01. Project Scope & Deliverables

All web and Android app development projects are delivered according to the selected package or agreed specification. Business websites launch within 48–72 hours, e-commerce stores within 4–7 days, and custom full-stack Android applications within 2–3 weeks.

02. Milestone Payments

Development projects operate on transparent milestones (typically a 50% kickoff deposit and 50% upon staging approval and deployment). Mentorship plans are billed monthly or per session block prior to live coaching.

03. 1-on-1 Developer Tutoring & Mentorship

Mentorship sessions are scheduled flexibly via Google Meet or Zoom. Students may reschedule a session with at least 12 hours' notice via WhatsApp. All code built by students during mentorship belongs 100% to the student for their portfolio.

04. Warranty & Support

Every delivered website and Android application includes a free 30-day technical warranty covering bug fixes, responsiveness adjustments, and deployment support.
