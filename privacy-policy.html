Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Last Updated: September 2026. At MalmaTech Software Agency, we strictly respect and prioritize the confidentiality of our clients, code assets, and project data across global jurisdictions.

01

Information We Collect

When you contact MalmaTech, submit an inquiry for our packages, or engage our web engineering services, we collect information necessary to complete your project:

  • Contact Details: Full name, company name, WhatsApp number, phone number, and official email address.
  • Project Specifications: Brand references, preferred design palettes, custom feature requirements, database architectures, and API endpoints.
  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymized website interactions, country selection, and selected quote currencies to optimize browser rendering and quote generation.
02

How We Use Your Information

We use collected client data strictly for purposeful engineering and operational needs:

  • Engineering, configuring, testing, and deploying your website or web application.
  • Setting up domain DNS records, SSL certificates, cloud hosting environments, and business email inboxes.
  • Processing milestone invoices and confirming payment receipts via Stripe, PayPal, M-Pesa, or Bank Wire.
  • Providing continuous technical support, bug patches, and post-launch SLA maintenance.
03

Non-Disclosure & Confidentiality

We hold client ideas, startup business logic, proprietary datasets, and API access keys in the strictest confidence under strict Non-Disclosure terms. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your data to third-party advertisers under any circumstances.

04

Third-Party Payment Processors & Infrastructure

Financial transactions are handled securely by PCI-DSS certified payment processors (Stripe, PayPal, Safaricom Daraja M-Pesa, and accredited bank networks). MalmaTech never stores your raw credit card numbers or banking passwords on our local servers.

05

Intellectual Property & Code Ownership

Upon fulfillment of final project milestones and complete payment settlement, 100% of all custom source code, frontend UI design files, custom backend scripts, and media deliverables are transferred exclusively to you. You retain full intellectual property rights to your product.
