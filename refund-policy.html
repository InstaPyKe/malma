Refund & Milestone Guarantee Policy

At MalmaTech, client satisfaction is our highest priority. We structure our software engineering projects around transparent milestones and explicit deliverables.

01

100% Satisfaction & Iteration Guarantee

During the design and staging review milestone, we work collaboratively with you to review the responsive layout, navigation, and features. We perform revisions until the website matches the agreed scope before final production deployment.

02

Deposit Refund Prior to Development

If you cancel a project before active design mockups or code architecture has commenced (within 24 hours of placing an order), your initial deposit is eligible for a full 100% refund, minus any standard bank or payment gateway processing fees.

03

Non-Refundable Third-Party Costs

Certain direct third-party expenses purchased explicitly on your behalf cannot be refunded once registered:

  • Custom domain registrations (e.g. .com, .co.ke, .org domain registrar fees).
  • Dedicated commercial SSL certificates or third-party paid font licenses.
  • Custom dedicated cloud server / VPS provisioning fees from cloud providers.
